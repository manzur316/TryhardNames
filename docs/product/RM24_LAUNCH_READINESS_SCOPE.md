# RM-24 Launch Readiness Scope

RM-24 implements the Launch Readiness Pack.

## Implements

- launch readiness overview;
- production smoke checklist;
- observability and monitoring checklist;
- rollback plan;
- launch go/no-go matrix;
- policy final review;
- roadmap updates;
- docs/source tests.

## Source Of Truth

GitHub/main/docs/product/merged PRs/CI.

Chat is not a source of truth.

## Non-Goals

- no deploy execution;
- no provider runtime;
- no OAuth/API activation;
- no `/cosmetics`;
- no store, checkout, billing, or payments;
- no DB migrations;
- no secrets or env vars;
- no remote Supabase, Vercel, Google Cloud, or Riot Portal changes.
